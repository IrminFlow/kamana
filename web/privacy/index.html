Information we collect and why

  • Profile details: name, state, city or PIN code, trade, job profile, experience, description, optional email and mobile number, to publish and search applicant profiles.
  • Mobile verification: your number and OTP session information are sent to Google Firebase Authentication to deliver and verify a one-time code, prevent abuse and confirm control of the number. Google reCAPTCHA runs when you request a code to block automated abuse. OTP and complaint rate-limit records use one-way keyed hashes of your number, email and IP address, never the raw values, and expire automatically.
  • Complaints: name, email, optional phone, complaint category, details and any profile link, to investigate, respond and keep a grievance record.
  • Essential technical information: a session cookie needed for security and form protection. We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

What is public

When you submit a profile, your name, location, trade, job profile, expected salary, specialisation, any email address you provide and verified mobile number are visible to people who find your profile. By clicking Submit, you confirm that you understand and agree to this display. Search results are limited to profiles matching the selected state and any trade or job profile filters provided, plus city or PIN code when provided. The WhatsApp button opens a WhatsApp chat using your verified number. Do not submit information you do not want public. OTP verification confirms access to a phone number; it does not verify identity, qualifications, work history or safety.

Who processes information for us

We use Vercel for website hosting, Convex for the database, Google Firebase (Google Cloud) for OTP delivery and Resend for complaint emails. They process information to provide their services to Kamana and may use infrastructure in locations where they operate, subject to applicable law and their own privacy terms. We may disclose information when required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to address security and safety concerns.

How long we keep it

Public profile information remains available until you delete your profile, ask us to remove it, or we remove it for safety, accuracy or legal reasons. Submitting a new profile with the same mobile number replaces the previous one. Rate-limit records are kept briefly for abuse prevention and expire automatically. Complaint records are retained while we handle the complaint and as reasonably necessary for legal, security and record-keeping purposes. Backups or records we must preserve by law may take longer to clear.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to access or correct your personal information, remove a profile, withdraw publication consent, or raise a privacy concern. Email info@kamana.in from the address used on your profile and include the profile name, location and mobile number so we can locate it. You can also file a tracked complaint on our Complaints page. We may ask for reasonable proof that you control the profile before changing or removing it. You can also use the contact details above.

Security and children

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, access controls and limited administrative access. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Kamana is intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly publish profiles submitted by children; contact us if you believe a child’s information has been submitted.

Changes

We may update this notice as the service or applicable law changes. The current version and effective date will be posted here. Material changes will be highlighted on the site where appropriate.
