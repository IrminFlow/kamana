Safety and Community Rules.

Effective 18 November 2026

Kamana is a directory that helps adults find job applicants. Profiles are automatically published after mobile OTP verification; OTP confirms control of a number only. Please use your own judgment and report concerns.

For people applying

  • Use your own name and contact details, and keep job, location and experience information truthful.
  • Any email address you provide and your verified mobile number are public to people who find your profile. Do not put sensitive personal information, identity numbers, bank details or private documents in your description.
  • Do not pay someone who promises a job, offer letter or interview without independently checking them. Do not share OTPs, passwords or bank credentials.

For people searching

  • Verify identity, qualifications, references, work eligibility and suitability independently.
  • Never ask for or share OTPs, passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary identity documents.
  • Arrange first meetings in a safe public place, tell someone you trust, and do not send advance payments without independent checks.
  • Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, spam, scrape or misuse a person’s public contact information.

Not allowed

Fraud, impersonation, false credentials, unlawful services, discrimination, harassment, threats, spam, non-consensual personal information, content involving exploitation of children, and attempts to misuse or disrupt Kamana are prohibited. Report a profile or concern through the Complaints page. Kamana may hide a profile while reviewing a report and may remove it or refer suspected unlawful activity to appropriate authorities.
